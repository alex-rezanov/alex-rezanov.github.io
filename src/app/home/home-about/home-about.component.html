My path to design wasn't linear. And that’s even better.
I’m naturally interested about the mechanics of how things work, curious human being. Lots of things that I’ve tried in my life are now shaping me as a professional. And I gladly use all of those experiences to produce thoughtful design work. My multidirectional background gave me a deep empathy for people and a creative way of thinking. It taught me to stay calm under pressure, cherish self-discipline, accept challenges, look several moves ahead and never settle. Design is where all these worlds meet, perfect space where I apply these traits to solve real-world problems.
